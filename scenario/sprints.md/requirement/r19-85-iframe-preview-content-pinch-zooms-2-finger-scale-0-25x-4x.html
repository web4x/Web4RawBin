R19.85: Iframe preview content pinch-zooms (2-finger scale 0.25x-4x).

Tron directive
Pinch gesture on the preview iframe container must scale the iframe content (transform: scale). Pinch-in shrinks, pinch-out grows. Clamp [0.25, 4.0]. Single-touch pan preserved. No snap-back on release. Currently no scale implementation exists.
