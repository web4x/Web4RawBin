SVG renders in fullscreen iframe natively

Tron directive
SVG diagrams at /md/<path>.svg render inside an iframe covering most of the viewport. Pan/zoom uses the browser native/default page zoom inside the iframe — no custom JS. The iframe isolation scopes zoom to the SVG, keeping the outer page static.
